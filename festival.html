Festivals Of Darbhanga

Darbhanga is famous for its traditional festivals and Mithila culture. People celebrate festivals with great joy, music, dance and devotion. Chhath Puja is the most important festival here. Durga Puja, Diwali, Holi and Sama Chakeva are also celebrated beautifully.
