History Of Darbhanga

Darbhanga is one of the oldest and most famous cities of Bihar. It is known as the heart of Mithila region. The city became famous during the rule of the Darbhanga Raj family, which was one of the richest royal families in India. Darbhanga has a rich cultural and historical heritage. The city is famous for Mithila paintings, Maithili language, traditional festivals, ancient temples, ponds and palaces. The famous Darbhanga Raj Palace, Ahilya Asthan, and historical forts attract many visitors every year. Darbhanga is also an important educational center of Bihar. Lalit Narayan Mithila University (LNMU) is one of the famous universities located here.
